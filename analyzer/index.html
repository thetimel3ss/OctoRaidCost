Consumables and prices

Gold per whole item; applications use the charge count. Blank manual prices restore the selected price source. Ingredient estimates require all ingredient quotations.

Ingredient quotations

Evidence and manual review

Needs review shows unresolved item identity or use evidence. Eligible effect-derived estimates are already included in estimated spending; inspect them under Effect-derived or All evidence. Missing prices are handled in the price editor.

Review changes apply to one grouped observation. Select an item and a physical-use quantity only when the evidence supports it. Manual decisions contribute to estimated cost. Generic food/drink observations can recur and must not be bulk-counted as items.

Coverage and limitations

Only direct item records or eligible timestamped casts/out-of-combat uses can contribute automatically. In-combat aura/heal-only records, active-at-pull buffs and ambiguous effects require review. Same-time direct and effect evidence is correlated; nearby uses are never merged by time alone. Range and omissions in the source log cannot be recovered.

History and comparison

Full-raid totals at each report’s original prices. Current player, encounter and category filters do not alter historical totals. Repeated snapshots of the same raid are shown separately.
